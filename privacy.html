JooseBooks Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 6, 2026**

Joose Labs ("we", "us") built JooseBooks as an offline bookkeeping app. This policy explains what information the app handles.

The short version

**JooseBooks collects nothing. Shares nothing. Sends nothing.**

Data storage

All data you enter into JooseBooks (amounts, categories, notes) is stored in a local database **on your device only**. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party. There is no server, no account, no cloud sync.

No tracking

JooseBooks contains no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and no third- party SDKs that collect information.

Data you can delete

You can delete individual entries in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all data permanently from your device.

Permissions

JooseBooks requests no special permissions. It works entirely offline.

Children

JooseBooks does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, because it collects no information at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this URL with an updated date. Since the app works offline, it will never transmit your data to us — there is no "us" on the receiving end.

Contact

Joose Labs — jooselabs@protonmail.com

*This policy applies to the JooseBooks mobile application distributed via Google Play. Last review: 2026-09-06.*

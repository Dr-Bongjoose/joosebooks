## Full description

Tired of accounting software that assumes you're an accountant?

JooseBooks does one thing: it keeps score of your money.

• MONEY IN, MONEY OUT — record an entry in three taps
• THE THREE NUMBERS — income, expenses, profit. That's the whole dashboard
• TAX SET-ASIDE — a suggested amount to set aside (~28%) so tax season never scares you
• YEAR AT A GLANCE — flip through years, watch the profit line grow
• CSV EXPORT — one file, hand it to your accountant, done
• 100% OFFLINE — your data never leaves your phone. No account, no cloud, no tracking

Built by Joose Labs for freelancers, side-hustlers, and anyone who bounced
off QuickBooks. No ledger entries, no double-entry, no jargon. Just the
numbers that matter.

Bookkeeping for people who hate bookkeeping.
